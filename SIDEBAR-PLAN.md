CRM SIDEBAR & MODULE ORGANIZATION UPDATE

Continue upgrading the existing Anthony Multiservice LLC CRM.

IMPORTANT:
Do not create duplicate modules, tables, pages, pipelines, or fields if an equivalent already exists.
Before creating anything new, inspect the current CRM structure and extend existing modules whenever possible.
Preserve all existing functionality, data, permissions, bilingual support, Client 360, Company 360, Payments, Referrals, Academy, Media Library, AI Team, and integrations.

==================================================
1. ADD / ORGANIZE THREE DISTINCT LEFT-SIDEBAR MODULES
==================================================

Add or confirm these three separate modules in the left navigation:
1. Communications / Comunicaciones
2. Community & Strategic Alliances / Comunidad y Alianzas Estratégicas
3. Social Media / Redes Sociales

These modules must remain separate because they serve different purposes.

==================================================
2. COMMUNICATIONS / COMUNICACIONES
==================================================

Purpose: Track direct communication with clients, leads, students, partners, and companies.

Channels: WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Phone Calls, Facebook Messenger, Instagram Direct Messages, Website Chat, HighLevel, In-Person, Other

Use the existing Communications Hub if already created. Do NOT create separate duplicate message modules for each channel.

Each communication must connect to: Client, Company, Service, Referral, Student, Partner, Task, Appointment, Assigned User.

Inside Client 360 and Company 360 show one unified communication timeline.

==================================================
3. COMMUNITY & STRATEGIC ALLIANCES
==================================================

Purpose: Manage human relationships, organizations, alliances, memberships, community partners, and B2B relationships. This module is NOT for social media messages.

Include: Churches, Chambers of Commerce, Business Associations, Latino Associations, Community Organizations, Professional Associations, CPA/Accountants, Attorneys, Insurance Agents, Realtors, Consultants, Financial Partners, Technology Partners, Referral Partners, Training Partners, Universities, Business Organizations, Other Strategic Alliances.

Create or reuse Partner 360/Organization records.

Fields: Organization Name, Contact Person, Organization Type, Phone, Email, Website, City, State, Country, Relationship Owner, Date Introduced, Membership Status, Agreement Status, Referral Agreement, Commission Agreement, Marketing Permission, Logo Permission, Services Connected, Last Contact, Next Follow-Up, Active/Inactive, Notes.

Statuses: Prospect, Contacted, Meeting Scheduled, Under Discussion, Agreement Review, Active Partner, Member, Paused, Inactive.

Dashboard metrics: Active Strategic Partners, New Partners, Meetings Scheduled, Referrals from Partners, Revenue from Partners, Associations by State, Chambers by State, Community Events, Follow-Ups Due.

Connect to: Referrals, Business Intelligence, Latino Business Map, Companies, Academy, Communications, Calendar, Tasks.

==================================================
4. SOCIAL MEDIA / REDES SOCIALES
==================================================

Purpose: Plan, organize, schedule, and track content for Anthony Multiservice LLC and related approved brands. This is NOT the same as Communications.

Platforms: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Website, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Channel, Other.

Use and connect to the existing Media Library. Do NOT create a second media storage system.

Each content record: Content ID, Title, Platform, Content Type, Campaign, Brand, Service, Audience, Language, Caption, Hashtags, Call to Action, Media File, Media Library ID, Approval Status, Scheduled Date, Published Date, Post URL, Performance Status, Created By, Approved By, Notes.

Content Types: Image, Video, Reel, Short, Story, Carousel, Live, Educational Post, Promotion, Testimonial, Event, Blog, Other.

Statuses: Idea, Draft, In Review, Approved, Scheduled, Published, Archived
Spanish: Idea, Borrador, En revisión, Aprobado, Programado, Publicado, Archivado

==================================================
5. SOCIAL MEDIA CALENDAR
==================================================

Create or extend a content calendar. Do NOT create a duplicate of the main client appointment calendar. Use a separate Content Calendar view connected to Social Media.

Allow: Day, Week, Month, Campaign View. Color-code content by platform or brand.

Filters: Platform, Campaign, Service, Brand, Status, Language, Assigned User, Date.

==================================================
6. SOCIAL MEDIA PERFORMANCE
==================================================

Prepare internal tracking fields for: Views, Reach, Impressions, Likes, Comments, Shares, Saves, Clicks, Leads Generated, Appointments Generated, Sales Generated.

Do not automatically fetch metrics until social APIs are connected. Prepare future integration with: Meta Facebook, Meta Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, HighLevel.

==================================================
7. APPROVAL & BRAND CONTROL
==================================================

Before any content is published, require approval if content includes: RRI Financial Group LLC, Strategic Partners, Financing Services, Immigration Services, Tax Claims, Testimonials, Client Images, Partner Logos.

Track: Approval Required, Approved By, Approval Date, Partner Approval Required, Partner Approval Status.

Do not publish partner names, logos, services, or claims without approval where required.

==================================================
8. LEFT SIDEBAR FINAL ORDER
==================================================

Dashboard, Clients, Companies, Services, Referrals, Payments, Finance, Communications, Community & Strategic Alliances, Social Media, Tasks, Calendar, Documents, Academy, Media Library, Government & Compliance, Business Intelligence, AI Team, Professional Systems, Websites, Reports, Settings

Keep bilingual labels with ES | EN selector.

==================================================
9. DUPLICATION RULES
==================================================

Communications = direct messages and contact history.
Community & Strategic Alliances = organizations, partners, memberships, community and B2B relationships.
Social Media = content planning, publishing preparation and performance.
Media Library = storage for images, videos and media assets.
Academy = students, courses, progress, certificates and educational operations.

Do not merge these modules. Do not duplicate them.

==================================================
10. SECURITY
==================================================

Admin: full access. Manager: all business modules. Communications Staff: communications only. Community Manager: community and alliances. Marketing Staff: social media + media library. Academy Staff: academy communications and academy media only. Referral Manager: partner/referral communications only.

Do not expose sensitive tax, immigration, banking, or client documents in Social Media or Community views.

==================================================
11. FINAL VALIDATION
==================================================

After implementation provide a report showing: existing modules reused, new modules added, sidebar updated, Communications preserved, Community & Strategic Alliances created or extended, Social Media created, Media Library reused, Content Calendar created, role permissions, dashboard metrics, any database migration required, any duplicate tables/pages avoided.
